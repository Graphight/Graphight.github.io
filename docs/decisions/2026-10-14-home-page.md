# Home page

- Status: Accepted
- Created: 2026-10-14
- Reflects: branch `home-page`, commits `1d7eea2` to `8d85aab`, built locally with the `github-pages` gem and the rendered `_site/index.html` read for the hero, three numbers, six timeline rows and the milestone, two articles, the publication and Space Probe; not yet checked by eye on a phone or a laptop; proposal `home-page`; the five home boards of the design canvas drawn on 2026-10-14 (link in decision `own-theme`)

## Summary
The home page is a hero with the name, a line and two buttons, three headline numbers, the work history timeline, then writing and projects side by side.
The question was: can the home page give a recruiter the numbers and the timeline in one scroll, without turning the site into a CV?
Built; whether it reads as a personal site rather than a CV is the eye check still to come.

## Context
`index.md` was three sentences on the `page` layout; its icon row went in decision `own-theme`, and the footer carries the social links.
The revenue and savings figures sat only in the resume PDF and in the prose of About.
The site's first job is to be a personal site; reading well to a recruiter is the second, and wanted only if it costs the first little.

## Design
- **`index.md` uses the `default` layout and holds its own sections in `.wrap`.**
  The hero is wider than the 900 px reading column, and proposal `hero-animation` draws in its right 57 %.
  No `home` layout, since one page would use it.
- **The hero holds a kicker (Machine learning · Physics · Games) in mono, the name in Bricolage at `clamp(56px, 12vw, 128px)`, one paragraph, and two buttons.**
  See my work is filled `kw` and jumps to `#work`; Resume (PDF) is outlined.
  The hero's minimum height is 580 px.
- **The paragraph is the previous `index.md` text, unchanged**, until Tom writes the line.
- **Three numbers sit under the hero as a `<dl>`**: €15M+ added annual revenue, $200K+ yearly egress savings, 0 → 1 for Pyper Vision.
  Each is already on the resume.
  The grid uses `auto-fit` with a 200 px minimum, so it wraps on a phone without a media query.
- **The timeline is `_includes/timeline.html`, unchanged**, so rows still open to their resume points, as on the Resume page.
  It is capped at the 900 px column width so opened points keep a readable measure.
- **Writing and projects are two columns above 720 px and stack below.**
  Writing lists the articles newest first from `site.pages` with the `article` layout, the loop `_layouts/article.html` already uses, then the 2024 publication; a new article appears without editing the home page.
  Projects lists Space Probe with the sentence from `pages/projects.md`.
  Each column ends with a link to its full page.

## Alternatives considered
- **Index's identity sidebar.** The most recruiter-friendly, and its two columns wrap poorly on a phone.
- **Cards for each employer**, as in Isobar. Three cards say less than a timeline and take twice the height.
- **Notebook's plain list.** The fallback if Field fails.
- **A compact timeline on the home page, without points.** A parameter on the include; left out because the points are closed by default, and the Resume page already shows the same rows.
- **A `home` layout.** One page would use it.

## Consequences & open questions
- On a phone the hero fills the first screen; cutting it to about 440 px is a one-value change if that reads as too much.
- Whether the numbers belong on a personal site at all is a matter of taste; they are the one part written for a recruiter.
- The numbers are a second hand-kept copy of figures in the resume PDF and `_data/experience.yml`.
- The kicker names games for Space Probe; the projects page has to keep up that claim.
- The publication and Space Probe are written into `index.md` by hand, as they are on Writing and Projects.
- The page has not been checked by eye on a phone or a laptop, in either mode.

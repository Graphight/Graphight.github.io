# Projects

Side project(s) I've built in my spare time.

## GD-SurvivalRTS - Coming Soon

A 2D co-op survival RTS built in Godot Engine, set in a Norse winter.
Push fires out into the snow, pay their upkeep in wood, and hold through blizzard after blizzard until Ragnarok.
Inspired by _They are Billions_ and _Age of Darkness: Final Stand_.

I am making this as I think this genre could do with more love and I wanted to improve upon the co-op experience introduced with Age of Darkness.

## VS-Siding - 2026-09-25

A Vintage Story mod for building thin, layered walls and floors instead of stacking full blocks.
Pick a framing, an infill and a finish for each face, anything from a bone and pelt tent to a weatherboarded house.
The code is on GitHub at [github.com/Graphight/VS-Siding](https://github.com/Graphight/VS-Siding) and the mod page can be found on their official ModBD: [https://mods.vintagestory.at/vssiding](https://mods.vintagestory.at/vssiding).

After playing with the VS-Roofing mod, it started to bother me that walls were not held to the same standard, so it felt like the obvious move was to create that mod.

## Space Probe - 2026-02-18

A small, simple, strategy game built in Godot Engine while between jobs. 
Manage resources, explore the galaxy, and fight off opposing Von Neumann probe fleets.
Play it on [itch.io](https://graphight.itch.io/space-probe).

I made this game while job hunting as something fun to get me through the grind.
I set the scope deliberately small so that I could actually ship a "complete" game (take that graveyard of unfinished projects) in a short time frame.
This was achieved in a little under a month of work. 
If you want to check out the code, I have made the repo public, [https://github.com/Graphight/space-probe](https://github.com/Graphight/space-probe).
